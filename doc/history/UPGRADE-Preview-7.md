DEVELOPMENT PREVIEW - BUILD 7

Readability and layout repair based on the four Windows screenshots.
- Full-width captions are laid out and centered after title updates.
- Three-column world setup: settings, landscapes, ready-made cities.
- 18px City Hall text and plain-language controls/explanations.
- Game speed: Pause / Normal / Fast / Fastest; framed right panel.
- Top-right Mayor button retained. You remain in charge.
- Sun and weather change automatically. Old forced modes reset when entering play.
- Per-sprite moonlight replaces the screen veil. Powered buildings stay brighter;
  fake floating windows removed. Dawn/dusk lighting rerenders the cached map.

MAYOR
You build by default; the mayor only gives advice. Let it help nearby, across the
city, or stop it. Nearby means a 25x25 area around the camera WHEN you assign it.
It checks monthly, placing at most one building or one connecting road tile.
Basic services allow water wells, schools, health centres and fire stations.
Parks and road-gap filling have their own permissions. The construction budget
resets yearly; running costs still come from the city treasury. $25,000 stays safe.

BUILDINGS
Warehouse stores and passes supplies between connected transport. Costs $6/day.
Tech campus turns steel into goods and raises technology; needs a university.
Cafe/food hall use food and goods and add city income when supplied. Cafe needs
four housing buildings; food hall needs eight per business. All require workers,
water and electricity except the warehouse.

LIMITS
Windows startup and rendered interaction for this build are not tested here.
The uploaded screenshots show Build 6 running, not this revised binary.
Real nighttime window artwork and streetlight assets remain unfinished; this
build changes sprite illumination, not the complete artwork set.
Full political simulation, self-learning AI and complete world-art overhaul remain
unfinished. Original simulation features and older documentation are retained.
