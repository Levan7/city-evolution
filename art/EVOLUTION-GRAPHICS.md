# City Evolution graphics

Four new isometric building sprites generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-11-01, then cropped/scaled with Pillow for engine integration. Alpha channels retained. Original game assets retain their original licenses.

Final game assets: `data/images/tiles/evolution-warehouse.png`, `evolution-tech.png`, `evolution-cafe.png`, `evolution-bistro.png`.

Shared prompt: Use case stylized-concept. Transparent isometric city-building sprite for LinCity-NG; detailed polished 3D city-building strategy game; realistic proportions; crisp architectural forms; soft ambient shadows within lot; orthographic isometric camera; 2:1 diamond ground projection; building centered; whole lot visible; neutral daylight from upper left; transparent background outside lot; no labels, watermark, UI or border; match classic 2.5D sprites with improved material detail.

Subjects:
- Modern freight transfer warehouse, loading docks, parked delivery truck, paved yard, rooftop solar panels; two-tile diamond lot.
- Modern technology research campus, glass facades, white concrete, rooftop antennas, trees, courtyard; three-tile diamond lot.
- Neighborhood cafe, warm brick, large windows, green awning, outdoor tables, planters; one-tile diamond lot.
- Contemporary food hall and bar, glazed frontage, warm lighting, rooftop patio and sidewalk tables; two-tile diamond lot.

The warehouse rooftop panels are decorative; this building is a freight buffer, not a power generator. Public-road vehicles and pedestrians are code-rendered decorative agents; inventory movement remains the original commodity simulation.
