# Preview 26 artwork
Airport and Grand Prix circuit sprites were generated for City Evolution and exported as transparent 768 × 384 PNGs under data/images/tiles.

The generation prompts requested complete square isometric grass footprints, 2:1 orthographic projection, warm brick buildings with green roofs, and the classic LinCity-NG game style. The airport includes a runway, terminal, tower and apron. The circuit includes a continuous winding track, curbs, pits and grandstand. Generated through the built-in image generator.
