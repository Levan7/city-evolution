# Preview 9 artwork

45 masters were generated with the built-in image generation tool and mechanically
trimmed/resized to PNG sprites. Earlier masters are carried forward from the art
review pack; new prompts are recorded in transit-prompts.json and additional-prompts.json.
The manifest lists all exports and 46 resource bindings covering 29 distinct models.
The old house master is retained but not assigned to shanties. Horse/cart/truck are
static decorative sprites, while directional bus, train and car sprites move.
Native SVG toolbar icons are carried forward from the reviewed icon pack; seven
new transport thumbnail/track icons were added. Brand mark and elevated track
geometry are drawn programmatically. All original assets retain their licenses.
This document supersedes older art notes for the assets listed in the manifest.
